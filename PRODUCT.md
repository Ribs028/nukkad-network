# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Indian and international travellers who want to understand a city through specific local recommendations rather than a generic tourism checklist. They may know the city name but need a trustworthy place to begin.

## Product Purpose

Nukkad Network helps someone choose any Indian city, discover evidence-backed local places, build a simple itinerary and eventually connect with a trusted local companion. Success means the visitor feels guided by a thoughtful local friend while every published recommendation remains traceable to its sources.

## Brand Personality

Warm, curious and street-smart. English leads for global clarity. Short Romanised Hindi lines add Indian character without blocking understanding.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic travel-listicle language, crowded tourism portals, glossy luxury-travel styling, unexplained slang and empty recommendations presented as facts. Avoid flat cream surfaces that feel unfinished.

## Design Principles

- Community evidence comes before confident recommendations.
- Indian character should welcome a global visitor, not exclude them.
- Exploration should feel playful while controls stay familiar and accessible.
- Every city can enter the system before its editorial guide is complete.
- Visual variety should reinforce place identity without reducing readability.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard access, visible focus states, reduced-motion support, legible contrast, useful image text and clear English labels are required. Decorative Hindi lines are optional context and never the only label for an action.
